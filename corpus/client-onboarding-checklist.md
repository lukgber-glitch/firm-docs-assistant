# Client Onboarding Checklist

*Fictional sample document of "Lindner & Vogt Steuerberatung (fictional)". Synthetic data.*

Every new client is onboarded in the same order. The responsible accountant ticks each item; the partner signs off at the end. Target duration from first meeting to full set-up: ten working days.

## 1. Before the first work
- Signed engagement letter on file.
- Identity check: a copy of a valid passport or ID card for each owner and managing director.
- Anti-money-laundering questionnaire completed, including the beneficial owners of the company.
- Power of attorney for the tax authority (tax filing authorisation) signed by the client.

## 2. Documents we need from the client
- Company registry extract (not older than three months) and articles of association.
- Last two annual financial statements and the last two tax assessments.
- Tax number, VAT number and the login details for the tax portal, handed over through the secure client portal, never by email.
- Bank statements for the current year and a list of open receivables and payables.
- For payroll clients: employee master data, current contracts and social insurance registrations.

## 3. Set-up on our side
- Create the client in the practice system and assign an accountant and a deputy.
- Open the client folder in the document management system with the standard folder structure.
- Enter all statutory deadlines of the client in the deadline calendar.
- Invite the client to the secure client portal and send the data-protection notice.

## 4. Kick-off call
Agree on how and when documents are delivered (monthly upload by the 5th working day), who the contact person is, and how invoices are approved.

## 5. Sign-off
The partner reviews the file for completeness. Only then is the client marked "active".
