## Our promise
We take complaints seriously and treat them as a chance to improve. Clients can complain about the quality of our work, the behaviour of staff, fees, or delays, without any disadvantage to their engagement.

## How to complain
Write to complaints@example.invalid, use the "Feedback" form in the client portal, or tell your accountant or partner directly. Please describe what happened, when it happened, and what outcome you expect.

## What happens next
1. **Acknowledgement:** We confirm receipt within two working days.
2. **Assignment:** The complaint goes to a partner who was not involved in the matter.
3. **Investigation:** The partner reviews the file and speaks to the staff involved. We aim to finish within 15 working days. If more time is needed we tell you why and when you can expect an answer.
4. **Answer:** You receive a written reply that explains the findings, any mistakes we found, and what we do about them, for example corrections at our cost or a fee reduction.
5. **Escalation:** If you are not satisfied, you may ask the managing partner to review the decision. You can also contact the professional chamber of tax advisers.

## Records
We log each complaint with date, topic and outcome. The log contains no more personal data than needed and is reviewed once a year in the partner meeting to find patterns.

## Errors that cause damage
If a complaint concerns an error that may have caused financial damage, we report it to our professional indemnity insurer without delay. This does not reduce your rights.
