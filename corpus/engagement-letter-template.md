# Engagement Letter Template (Standard Mandate)

*Fictional sample document of "Lindner & Vogt Steuerberatung (fictional)". Synthetic data.*

## Purpose
This template is used for every new client engagement. The partner in charge fills in the scope, the start date and the fee arrangement and sends it to the client for signature before any work begins. No work is started and no tax authority is contacted before the signed letter is on file.

## Scope of services
The engagement letter lists exactly which services are covered. The standard modules are: (1) bookkeeping and monthly VAT returns, (2) annual financial statements, (3) corporate and personal income tax returns, (4) payroll accounting, and (5) general tax advice. Services that are not listed, such as representation in a tax audit or in appeal proceedings, require a separate written extension of the mandate.

## Client duties
The client undertakes to provide complete and accurate documents by the agreed dates. The firm does not verify the factual correctness of the documents supplied. If documents arrive late, statutory deadlines may be missed through no fault of the firm, and the firm may charge for the extra effort of rush processing.

## Term and termination
The engagement runs for an indefinite period. Either party may terminate it in writing with one month's notice to the end of a calendar month. Termination does not affect fees already earned. Details on handing over files are in the document "Beendigung des Mandats (Mandatsende)".

## Liability
Liability for negligent breach of duty is limited to the amount covered by the firm's professional indemnity insurance, as far as the law allows. The client receives a copy of the insurance confirmation on request.

## Signature
The letter is signed by the responsible partner and by the client (or an authorised representative). A scanned copy is stored in the client file; the original goes to the client.
