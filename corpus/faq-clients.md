## How do I send you my documents?
Please upload them to the secure client portal. Sending sensitive documents by ordinary email is not allowed under our data-protection rules. Monthly bookkeeping documents are due by the 5th working day of the following month.

## Can I reach my accountant by phone?
Yes. Every client has a main contact and a deputy. Calls are answered during office hours; outside office hours, please leave a message and we call back within one working day.

## How quickly do you answer emails?
We answer within two working days. Urgent matters involving a deadline should be marked "URGENT" in the subject line.

## Can you take over from my previous tax adviser?
Yes. With your written consent we ask your previous adviser for the handover of your files. The first step is the onboarding checklist and a signed engagement letter.

## What does it cost to get started?
There is no set-up fee. Costs depend on the services in your engagement letter; see the fee policy. We give a non-binding fee estimate on request.

## Do you represent me during a tax audit?
Yes, but representation in an audit is not part of the standard mandate and needs a separate written extension of the engagement.

## Can I get my originals back?
Yes. We return original documents after the work is finished and keep copies for the legal retention period.

## What happens if you miss a deadline?
We have a four-eyes deadline control and a deadline calendar for every client. If a deadline is missed through our fault, please contact your partner at once; our liability is described in the engagement letter and covered by our professional indemnity insurance.

## Do you offer advice on cryptocurrency or foreign property?
Only if the engagement letter explicitly covers it. Please ask your partner before you buy.
