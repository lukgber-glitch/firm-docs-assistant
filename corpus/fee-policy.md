## Hourly rates
Work that is not covered by a flat fee is billed by time in 15-minute units. The current rates, excluding VAT, are:

| Role | Rate per hour |
|------|---------------|
| Partner | EUR 220 |
| Senior accountant | EUR 150 |
| Accountant | EUR 110 |
| Administrative support | EUR 65 |

## Flat fees
For recurring work we agree a monthly or annual flat fee in the engagement letter. A typical monthly bookkeeping package for a small company (up to 80 documents per month) costs EUR 290 per month. Annual financial statements for a small limited company start at EUR 1,800. A flat fee covers only the services named in the engagement letter.

## Expenses
Court and registry fees, courier costs and travel outside the city are charged at cost. Printing and postage are included in our fees.

## Invoicing and payment
Hourly work is invoiced monthly in arrears. Flat fees are invoiced at the start of each month. Invoices are payable within 14 days without deduction. After 30 days we send a reminder; from the second reminder we charge default interest of 4 percentage points above the base rate.

## Advance payments
For new clients without a track record with us we may ask for an advance payment of up to two monthly flat fees. The advance is credited against the final invoice.

## Rush work
If a client delivers documents after the agreed date and asks for the deadline to still be met, a rush surcharge of 25 percent applies to the hours worked.

## Fee estimates
On request we provide a non-binding estimate in writing. If it becomes clear that the estimate will be exceeded by more than 20 percent, we inform the client before continuing.
