# Internal Memo: Remote Work and Client Data

*Fictional sample document of "Lindner & Vogt Steuerberatung (fictional)". Synthetic data.*

**To:** All staff  **From:** Office management  **Subject:** Working from home with client data

## Rule in one sentence
Client data stays inside our systems: staff may work from home on two days per week, but only on firm laptops connected through the firm VPN.

## What is allowed
- Opening client files in the practice system and document management system through the VPN.
- Video calls with clients, using the firm's approved conferencing tool only.
- Printing at home is not allowed. If a paper copy is necessary, print it in the office.

## What is not allowed
- Saving client documents on private devices, USB sticks or private cloud storage.
- Forwarding client documents to private email addresses.
- Working on client data over public wifi without the VPN, for example in cafés or trains.
- Letting family members use a firm laptop.

## Screens and calls
Lock your screen whenever you leave the laptop. When you speak to a client by phone at home, make sure no one else can overhear the conversation.

## Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the office management immediately, at the latest within one hour of noticing. The device is blocked remotely. A report to the data-protection authority has to be made within 72 hours if personal data was at risk, so speed matters.

## Approval
Home-office days are agreed with the team lead in the shared calendar. This memo takes effect on 1 January 2027.
